### TalentForge AI — AI-Powered Technical Talent Assessment

TalentForge AI is a project focused on helping organizations assess technical candidates through configurable assessments and browser-based engineering simulations.

**Platform goals include:**
- Organization-aware assessment management and role-based templates.
- Candidate invitations and browser-based coding/engineering environments.
- Simulated developer tools such as codebases, terminals, logs, APIs, and monitoring views.
- Automated and AI-assisted evaluation with reports for comparing candidates.

**Repository:** [navodsamarakoon/talentforge-ai](https://github.com/navodsamarakoon/talentforge-ai)

*The bullets above describe the intended platform scope; feature availability may vary while development continues.*
